avwx-bot Privacy Policy

Effective October 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information avwx-bot (the "Bot") and its configuration website (together, the "Service") collect, how it is used, and how long it is kept. The Service is provided by its maintainer (the "Operator", "we", "us"). It is provided as is, as described in the Terms of Service, and we make no claim that this Policy is complete or legally sufficient for any particular jurisdiction.

1. Information we collect

Server configuration

When a server administrator configures the Bot, we store that server's settings: its Discord server ID, and the IDs of the channels and roles chosen. We keep the last 20 versions of the settings, each with the Discord user ID of the person who applied it, so a change can be undone.

Records of the Bot's own posts

To avoid duplicate posts and to update its earlier ones, the Bot records which morning briefs and weather alerts it has posted (by channel and message ID), and which forum threads it created and the airports they cover.

Conversations with the assistant

Where a server has the conversational assistant enabled, the Bot reads and stores the messages in a conversation thread while that conversation is open: the text of each message, the author's display name, and when it was sent, together with the assistant's replies and the data it looked up. A conversation starts when a member mentions the Bot in a channel the server has chosen for it, and stays open until the thread has been quiet for the period the server sets. We also record, per member and per day, how many requests each member made and their cost, to enforce usage limits.

Messages we do not keep

Discord delivers messages to the Bot from channels it can see. The Bot ignores, and does not store, any message that is not part of an open conversation or a mention of the Bot in a chosen channel. Slash commands are processed to answer them and are not stored, apart from the configuration changes described above.

Operational logs

The Bot keeps technical logs for troubleshooting. They may include server, channel, thread and user IDs (for example, who changed a configuration) and error details. They do not record message text.

The configuration website

The configuration website runs in your browser. Your server's settings and its channel and role names travel in the part of the link after "#", which browsers do not send to the web server. The site uses no cookies or analytics of its own. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log visits as described in its own privacy statement.

2. How information is used

Information is used only to provide the Service: to post briefs and alerts, to answer commands and questions, to keep conversations in context, to enforce usage limits, and to diagnose problems. We do not sell information, use it for advertising, or share it except as described below.

3. Who information is shared with

  • Discord delivers and displays all messages to and from the Bot, under Discord's Privacy Policy.
  • Anthropic provides the AI model behind the conversational assistant. The contents of an open conversation, including members' messages and display names, are sent to Anthropic's API to generate replies, and are handled under Anthropic's commercial terms and privacy policy. Nothing is sent to Anthropic from servers where the assistant is not enabled.
  • Weather data providers (aviationweather.gov and the National Weather Service) receive requests for airports, locations and products. These requests do not include information about Discord users.
  • We may disclose information if required by law.

4. How long information is kept

  • Conversations with the assistant: deleted 30 days after the conversation closes.
  • Records of closed weather alerts: deleted 7 days after the alert ends.
  • Server settings, the last 20 versions of them, records of morning briefs and created threads, and usage counts: kept while the Bot is in the server.
  • When the Bot is removed from a server, everything stored for that server is deleted.
  • Operational logs: kept as long as needed for troubleshooting.

5. Your choices

You can avoid the conversational assistant by not mentioning the Bot and not posting in its conversation threads. Server administrators can switch the assistant off, and can remove the Bot from their server, which deletes the server's stored data. To ask about or request deletion of information relating to you, open an issue as described below; please do not include personal information in a public issue.

6. Children

The Service is intended for users who meet Discord's minimum age requirements. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

7. Security

Data is stored on a privately operated server with reasonable precautions, but no system is completely secure and we cannot guarantee the security of any information.

8. Changes

We may update this Policy. The effective date above shows the latest version, and continued use of the Service after a change means you accept it.

9. Contact

Questions about this Policy can be raised as an issue at github.com/stellarhopper/avwx-bot.
